GBIF Backbone → COL 26.5 XR mapping — analysis

Review of gbif-col265.tsv — 7,746,724 rows, 30 columns, ~3.9 GB. Basic match metrics, matchType / matchIssues distributions, and patterns behind the no-matches.

UNITE SH OTUs excluded. 609,269 rows are UNITE Species Hypothesis OTU identifiers used as names (gbif:scientificName matching ^SH\d+\.\d+FU, e.g. SH220494.07FU, rank unranked). The version suffix differs between GBIF and COL, so all 609,269 are no-matches by construction (100%). They are removed from every metric below. Working total: 7,137,455 rows.

0. Identifier-as-name artefacts

Three families of “names” are really external identifiers. They behave very differently against COL:

ArtefactDetectionRowsNo-matchVerdict
UNITE SH OTUs^SH\d+\.\d+FU609,269100%Excluded — version differs (GBIF 09 vs COL 10), can never match.
BOLD BINs^BOLD:617,1600.4%Kept — present in COL, match almost perfectly. Not a no-match source.
GTDB placeholderskingdom Bacteria/Archaea AND (name has a digit OR ends _<CAPS>)40,473100%Deterministic non-matches; not wanted in COL.
  • BOLD: 617,160 BIN-as-name rows exist and 99.6% map to COL (only 2,391 misses). A non-issue.
  • GTDB: prokaryote nomenclature (ICNP) never uses digits, so within Bacteria/Archaea any name with a digit — or a _A/_B-style suffix — is a GTDB placeholder. This catches uninomial codes (ARS69, CAIJMQ01, 2-02-FULL-45-22_A), pure _CAPS splits (Bacillus_D, Myxococcota_A, Desulfobacterota_C) and binomial placeholders (… sp002774085, 28,514 of them). By rank: species 28,565, genus 8,877, family 1,919, order 818, class 202, phylum 92. By kingdom: Bacteria 37,311, Archaea 3,162.
  • GTDB placeholders explain 79% of all Bacteria/Archaea no-matches (40,473 of 51,127). The remainder are pure-letter GTDB names with no digit or suffix and so cannot be detected by pattern — this count is a lower bound.

4. Takeaways

  • After removing the 609k UNITE SH OTUs (a deterministic, expected miss), the match rate is 81.4%; matchIssue flags are negligible.
  • ~47% of matches resolve to a COL synonym/ambiguous/misapplied name — use col:acceptedID, not col:ID, for those.
  • Identifier-as-name artefacts account for a large, deterministic chunk of the misses: UNITE SH OTUs (609k, removed) and GTDB placeholders (40k) are simply not meant to be in COL. BOLD BINs (617k) are not a problem — they match.
  • Remaining (real) no-matches are systematic, concentrated in:
    1. provisionally accepted backbone names — 64% miss (auto-generated names COL doesn't carry);
    2. microbial lineages — Bacteria 66% and Archaea 80%, but ~79% of those are GTDB placeholders; the true unexplained prokaryote residual is only ~10.6k rows;
    3. algae 71% and Chromista 43% (protist/algal checklists less covered);
    4. high ranks (phylum 56%, class 37%, order 35%) — small counts, poorly aligned;
    5. genus-level names (27%) and incertae sedis (79%).
  • Curated animal groups (diptera 6.6%, lepidoptera 8.9%, hymenoptera 11.4%) and fungi-once-OTUs-are-removed (11.5%) match well — the gaps track the loosely-sourced parts of the old backbone that COL's vetted sources don't cover.
