Data model

Solid boxes are Catalogue of Life types; dashed are external. Taxa carry the classification through schema:parentTaxon and cite their source dataset with schema:isPartOf. Names hold the cross-links out to ITIS, BOLD, MolluscaBase and Index Fungorum. GBIF backbone identifiers point in to taxa via schema:sameAs.

Conventions

schema:Taxon covers every name usage, not just accepted ones — filter on dwc:taxonomicStatus, and note "provisionally accepted" is distinct from "accepted". Ranks are capitalised strings ("Species"). Dates use mixed datatypes — xsd:gYear, xsd:date, xsd:gYearMonth — so compare with SUBSTR(STR(?y),1,4), not ?y > 2015.

Status

Read-only, unauthenticated, best effort, running on a small machine. Please keep queries bounded.
